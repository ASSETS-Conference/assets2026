ASSETS 2026 Accessibility FAQ

ASSETS 2026 is committed to providing an inclusive environment. We will do our best to accommodate all accessibility requests. This page describes our accessibility provisions to help you decide whether the conference will work for you. If your question is not answered here, please contact us — we will work with you to the best of our ability.

Contact the Accessibility Chairs at any time: access-assets26@acm.org

Communicating Accessibility Needs

How do I communicate my accessibility needs to ASSETS 2026 organizers?

The Accessibility Chairs' goal is to ensure the conference is accessible to everyone. When you register, you will be asked: "Do you have a disability or special need which requires our awareness?" Please describe any accessibility needs — wheelchair access, sign language interpretation, dietary requirements, sensory sensitivities, or anything else. The Accessibility Chair will follow up to clarify your needs. You can also contact us directly at any time: access-assets26@acm.org .

What if my question is not answered here?

Contact the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org . For venue-specific accessibility questions (including accommodation rooms), you can also contact the Hilton Porto Gaia directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com .

Interpretation and Captioning

Will sign language interpretation or captioning be available?

Yes. The conference will provide sign language interpreters or CART captioners if requested during early registration. Please make your request as early as possible — we cannot guarantee availability for requests made after the early registration deadline. Contact access-assets26@acm.org to discuss your needs in detail.

Will there be an induction loop in the meeting rooms?

Please contact the Hilton Porto Gaia directly (call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com ) or the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org to confirm what hearing assistance technology will be available. If an induction loop is not available, we will work with you to find alternative accommodations.

Will speakers and questioners be using a microphone?

Yes. All speakers will use microphones. Audience members asking questions will be requested to use a microphone so that their question is heard clearly by all attendees and captured by any captioning system in use.

Getting to the Conference

What are the transport options for getting to the conference venue?

The easiest way to get to the Hilton Porto Gaia from Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (Porto Airport) is by taxi. Taxis are available at the taxi rank outside the arrivals terminal and the journey takes approximately 20–30 minutes depending on traffic. Uber also operates in Porto and can be booked via the app. Participants can enter the hotel through the main entrance or, for easier access, through the hotel parking area immediately adjacent to the main entrance (approved by the venue) — drivers can communicate at the exit gate that they are dropping off an ASSETS 2026 participant. If you require an accessible vehicle, please request one when booking or ask the dispatcher at the taxi rank. For further questions about transport, contact the Hilton Porto Gaia directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com .

Who can I ask about local accessibility?

The conference is hosted at the Hilton Porto Gaia, Porto, Portugal. For venue-specific accessibility questions, contact the hotel directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com . For general conference accessibility questions, contact the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org .

The Conference Venue

What is the conference space like?

All conference sessions take place within the hotel. Elevators are available and large enough to accommodate wheelchairs and power scooters. Paths between all session rooms are level and obstacle-free, with short distances between sessions and keynotes. Accessible restrooms are located near the conference rooms. The registration desk is fully accessible. A map of the conference space with a text description will be provided on the conference website closer to the event.

Will the conference be accessible by wheelchair or power scooter?

Yes. The main entrance has a ramp (please note there is some cobblestone at the entrance). Elevators are large enough for wheelchairs and power scooters. All routes between session rooms are level and obstacle-free. Dedicated wheelchair spaces are provided: the front row of the main auditorium is reserved for wheelchair users, and other rooms have clearly marked open spaces. The stage is being made fully accessible by the venue’s audiovisual team; a solution for the main auditorium’s taller stage is being finalized by the venue. Accessible restrooms are located near all conference rooms. For further wheelchair accessibility questions, contact the Hilton Porto Gaia directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com . You can also contact the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org .

How much walking or standing will be required?

All conference sessions are held within the hotel, with short distances between sessions and keynotes. Routes throughout are level and obstacle-free. Seating is available in all session rooms. If you need more specific information about the layout, please contact access-assets26@acm.org .

Will there be a designated quiet space?

We are working to provide a quiet space at the conference. This requires additional room availability and is still being confirmed with the venue — we expect to have an update closer to the conference date. Please contact access-assets26@acm.org if a quiet space is important for your attendance so we can keep you informed.

Will the conference be a smoke-free environment?

Yes. The Hilton Porto Gaia is a smoke-free hotel. Smoking is not permitted in any indoor conference areas. For further questions, contact the hotel directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com . You can also contact the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org .

Are there accessible hotel rooms available?

Yes. The Hilton Porto Gaia has a limited number of accessible bedrooms (3 rooms). We strongly recommend booking early. You will need to make a special accessibility request through the hotel’s normal reservation process. To arrange this, contact the hotel directly: call +351 22 244 9200 or email info@hiltonportogaia.com . Booking details will also be provided on the conference website. If you need assistance, contact access-assets26@acm.org .

Assistance and Support

Can a student volunteer assist me during the conference?

Yes. A dedicated team of trained accessibility student volunteers will be available throughout the conference to assist with navigation, locating sessions, meal service, and other needs. Please indicate on your registration form if you will need volunteer assistance. If you are bringing a personal care assistant, please email treasurer-assets26@acm.org to arrange complimentary registration for them.

What are the arrangements for presenters with accessibility needs?

Presenters who need any accommodation should contact the Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org as early as possible. Each session room will have at least 3 chairs and a desk at the stage for presenters who prefer to present while seated. An adjustable podium is also available. Stage access is fully accessible. We can additionally arrange slide advancement support, additional time, sign language interpretation, or any other reasonable accommodation. A student volunteer can assist you onto and off the stage if needed.

Can I bring my guide dog or service animal?

Yes. Guide dogs and other service animals are welcome at ASSETS 2026. A relief area with water will be available. Please indicate on your registration form if you are bringing a service animal so we can confirm the location and facilities in advance.

Can I attend the conference remotely by robot?

We are sorry, but ASSETS 2026 will not be able to accommodate remote attendance by robot.

Food and Dietary Needs

What food will be provided at the conference?

The conference will provide buffet meals and refreshments at breaks. All dishes will be clearly labelled including allergen information. If you have special dietary needs, please indicate these on your registration form.

Can the conference accommodate special dietary needs?

Yes. Attendees who indicate dietary requirements on their registration form will be provided with appropriate meals when the standard conference catering cannot accommodate them. If you have a severe allergy, please indicate this clearly on your form and follow up with access-assets26@acm.org to ensure we have the details noted.

Sensory and Medical

Will there be loud music, strobe lighting, or other strong sensory experiences?

Flash photography and strong scents are not permitted in conference sessions. Conference room lighting is adjustable. We are still gathering information about sensory elements of any associated social events. Please contact access-assets26@acm.org if you have specific sensory sensitivities so we can advise in advance.

What if I have a medical emergency during the conference?

In case of emergency, call 112 (the Portuguese emergency services number) for an ambulance or police. For non-urgent medical needs, contact our Accessibility Chairs at access-assets26@acm.org or ask any student volunteer — they can direct you to local medical services. For non-resident attendees, purchasing travel insurance that covers medical expenses is strongly recommended.

Discord as the Hybrid Platform

ASSETS 2026 will use Discord as the hybrid platform for communication throughout the conference. Discord is available through web browsers and desktop/mobile apps. The organizing committee will send out Discord registration instructions through email in October, closer to the conference.

We understand Discord has some accessibility tradeoffs, but after careful evaluation, we believe this platform is still the most appropriate for ASSETS. The following links are resources for accessibility support on Discord: Discord Accessibility and Accessibility Settings Tab .

If you anticipate any other accessibility barriers using Discord, please contact the Accessibility Chairs ( access-assets26@acm.org ) or Hybrid Chairs ( hybrid-assets26@acm.org ) for support.
