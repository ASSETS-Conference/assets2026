Author Instructions for Accepted ASSETS 2026 Contributions

These instructions give an overview over the publication-ready process that all authors of accepted ASSETS 2026 contributions need to follow. They apply to the following tracks: Technical Papers, Posters and Demonstrations, Experience Reports, Workshops (organizers' proposals, not individual attendee contributions), Doctoral Consortium, Student Research Competition. As the publication timeline differs for each individual track, please refer to the individual track pages and/or the email instructions that you received for submission dates.

Step 1: Preparing the Camera-Ready Version of Your Contribution

If you have promised changes in a rebuttal or received reviewer feedback that needs to be incorporated into the final version of your contribution, update your document accordingly, ensuring that you use one of the templates communicated via the individual track page.

Then you will need to prepare an accessible source file and an accessible PDF for upload to PCS, and ensure that you make that upload by the deadline relevant for your track.

  1. Producing an accessible source file. Please ensure alt-text is included in your source files. Every figure should have a text description unless it is purely decorative. Do not repeat the figure caption, but instead add a description that conveys the content of the figure to someone who cannot see it. Please refer to the ACM SIGACCESS Guidance on Describing Figures for best practices and examples. To include alt-text in your source files, please follow the instructions for Microsoft Word or LaTeX in Overleaf.
  2. Producing an accessible, tagged PDF. Please note that all camera-ready submissions must be formatted as a tagged PDF and follow the ACM SIGACCESS Accessible PDF Author Guide.

All submissions will be checked by Program Committee members and/or track chairs to ensure that all requested changes have been made.

Step 2: ACM e-Rights Form

The link to the ACM e-Rights Form will be emailed from rightsreview@acm.org to the contact author within a week of the acceptance notification for your contribution. Here, you need to confirm the paper title, author names, email addresses, and affiliations. You also need to submit ORCID IDs, which are mandatory for all authors. Please note that the information provided via e-Rights overwrites information provided in your source file (e.g., author names and affiliation), so it is important that you double-check that all information recorded in this form is correct. The completion of the ACM e-Rights Form triggers Step 3, so please ensure that the contact author is responsive in the time immediately after acceptance of your contribution, and fills in the form as soon as possible.

Step 3: Uploading Your Source Files to TAPS

After completing the ACM e-Rights Form, you will be contacted by Aptara to upload the accessible source file for your contribution to TAPS. Communication throughout the production process is handled by Aptara, so you should pay close attention to emails from them. While the accessibility tagging itself is carried out by Aptara, you should ensure that alt text for your figures and tables are included in your source files. Once the processed file is returned to you, it is important to review it for content accuracy, formatting, and accessibility. At this stage, you also have the optional opportunity to revise or improve the accessibility tagging before returning the file to Aptara. A one-week turnaround period is typically provided for this review process, and it requires you to be available at short notice once Aptara gets back to you. Once you have approved the author version, Aptara will handle the handover of your contribution to the ACM for publication in the Digital Library.

Step 4: Supplementary Material

Should your contribution include supplementary material, please prepare these by the deadline indicated for your track, and upload all files combined into a ZIP archive to PCS. To make the upload, you can use the same camera-ready form that you used to submit your PDF contribution in Step 1 - a part of that form will remain unlocked for a longer period to make the upload of additional materials possible. Please note that supplementary material must be accessible. To achieve this, we invite you to refer to the various accessibility guides provided by SIGACCESS. All submitted files will be checked by the ASSETS organizing committee and then shared with the ACM for inclusion in the Digital Library.
